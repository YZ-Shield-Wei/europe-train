Why Choose Night Trains?

Night trains in Europe combine transportation and accommodation, saving you both time and money. Instead of losing a day to travel, you sleep through the journey and arrive refreshed at your destination. Popular among backpackers, families, and business travelers alike, night trains offer:

  • Time efficiency: Travel while you sleep, maximizing your daytime for sightseeing
  • Cost savings: One ticket covers both transport and accommodation
  • Comfort: Private cabins with beds, washbasins, and even showers on premium services
  • Sustainability: Rail travel produces significantly less CO₂ than flying
  • Experience: A romantic, old-Europe travel tradition

Major Night Train Routes

ÖBB Nightjet (Austria)

The largest night train network in Europe, operated by Austrian Federal Railways. Key routes include:

  • Vienna → Zurich (via Innsbruck)
  • Vienna → Venice/Rome
  • Munich → Milan/Venice
  • Hamburg → Zurich
  • Berlin → Vienna

SNCF Intercités de Nuit (France)

French overnight services connecting Paris to southern destinations:

  • Paris → Nice (French Riviera)
  • Paris → Toulouse
  • Paris → Latour-de-Carol (Pyrenees)

SJ EuroNight (Scandinavia)

Connecting Sweden, Norway, and Denmark:

  • Stockholm → Narvik (Arctic Circle)
  • Stockholm → Berlin
  • Copenhagen → Stockholm

What to Expect On Board

  • Check-in: Arrive 30 minutes early; staff will show you to your cabin
  • Luggage: Store large bags in the corridor racks; keep valuables in your cabin
  • Facilities: Toilets at the end of each carriage; some cabins have private washbasins
  • Food: Bring snacks and water; some trains have dining cars or breakfast included
  • Security: Cabins lock from the inside; staff patrol the train throughout the night

Travel Tips

  • Pack light: Cabin space is limited; a small backpack is ideal
  • Bring earplugs: Trains can be noisy, especially at stops
  • Eye mask: Cabin lights may stay on at stations
  • Wear comfortable clothes: You'll be sleeping in your travel outfit
  • Set an alarm: Staff wake you, but it's good to have a backup

Frequently Asked Questions

Are night trains safe?

Yes, European night trains are generally very safe. Cabins lock from the inside, and staff patrol regularly. However, always secure your valuables and use common sense precautions.

Can I use a Eurail Pass on night trains?

Yes, most night trains accept Eurail passes, but you'll need to pay a supplement for the cabin (€10-€50 depending on type). Seat reservations are mandatory.

Do night trains have WiFi?

Most modern night trains offer WiFi, though quality varies. ÖBB Nightjet provides free WiFi in all cabin types.

What if I miss my stop?

Staff typically wake passengers before their destination. If you do miss your stop, speak to the conductor immediately—they'll help you arrange return transport.
