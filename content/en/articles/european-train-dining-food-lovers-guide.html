European Train Dining: A Food Lover's Guide to Onboard Cuisine 2026

European train travel isn't just about getting from A to B—it's a culinary journey. From gourmet dining cars to regional specialties, here's your complete guide to eating well on European trains.

Best Trains for Food Lovers

🇫🇷 TGV InOui - France

  • Dining: Full-service restaurant car with chef-prepared meals
  • Specialties: Three-course meals with wine pairings
  • Price: €15-35 for main courses
  • Tip: Reserve dining car seats in advance for lunch/dinner services

🇩🇪 ICE - Germany

  • Dining: Bordbistro with hot meals, snacks, and beverages
  • Specialties: German sausages, pretzels, local beers
  • Price: €8-18 for hot meals
  • Tip: Try the seasonal menu featuring regional German cuisine

🇨🇭 SBB - Switzerland

  • Dining: SBB Restaurant with Swiss specialties
  • Specialties: Rösti, cheese fondue, Swiss chocolate
  • Price: CHF 18-32 for main courses
  • Tip: Glacier Express offers the ultimate scenic dining experience

🇮🇹 Trenitalia - Italy

  • Dining: FrecciaBistro on high-speed trains
  • Specialties: Italian coffee, panini, pasta dishes
  • Price: €6-15 for meals
  • Tip: Business class includes complimentary drinks and snacks

🇦🇹 ÖBB - Austria

  • Dining: DoNado restaurant car on Railjet trains
  • Specialties: Wiener Schnitzel, Apfelstrudel, Austrian wines
  • Price: €12-25 for main courses
  • Tip: Breakfast service is excellent value at €8-12

Money-Saving Tips

  1. Bring your own: Most trains allow outside food—pack a picnic from local markets
  2. Book dining in advance: Some premium dining cars require reservations
  3. Travel off-peak: Dining cars less crowded, better service
  4. Use loyalty programs: BahnBonus, SBB rewards offer dining discounts

FAQ

Can I bring my own food on European trains?

Yes! Most European trains allow outside food and drinks. Some premium services may restrict alcohol.

Are dining cars included in all train tickets?

No, dining cars are available but meals must be purchased separately unless you're in premium classes with included catering.

What's the best train for a foodie?

The Glacier Express (Switzerland) and TGV InOui (France) offer the most premium dining experiences.
