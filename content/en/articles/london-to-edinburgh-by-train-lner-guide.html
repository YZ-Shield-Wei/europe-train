London to Edinburgh by Train: The Ultimate LNER Guide 2026

Traveling from London to Edinburgh by train is one of the UK's most popular rail journeys. With LNER's modern Azuma fleet, this 4.5-hour trip combines comfort, speed, and stunning scenery across the British countryside.

Route Overview

  • Distance: 632 km (393 miles)
  • Journey Time: 4h 20m - 4h 40m (direct)
  • Operator: LNER (London North Eastern Railway)
  • Train Type: LNER Azuma (Class 800/801)

LNER Azuma Experience

The Azuma trains offer:

  • Free WiFi and power sockets at every seat
  • Onboard catering with hot meals
  • Spacious seating with generous legroom
  • Quiet zones for peaceful travel

Stations

Scenic Highlights

The route passes through:

  • York - Historic cathedral city
  • Newcastle - Iconic bridges and quayside
  • Berwick-upon-Tweed - Coastal views
  • Scottish Borders - Rolling hills and countryside

FAQ

How long is the train from London to Edinburgh?

Direct LNER services take approximately 4 hours 20 minutes.

What's the cheapest way to travel London to Edinburgh?

Book Advance tickets 8+ weeks ahead, prices start from £25. Railcards offer additional savings.

Are there direct trains?

Yes, LNER operates frequent direct services throughout the day.
