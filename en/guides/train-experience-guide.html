On board European trains

The experience depends on the train, equipment and fare you select. Check the facilities for the actual departure rather than assuming that every first-class ticket includes meals or every train has unlimited luggage space.

Editorial guidance reviewed against the official sources below on 30 October 2026. Prices and available departures must be checked for your travel date.

Seats and travel classes

First and second class describe different products across operators. Inspect the departure’s seat plan, quiet or family areas and accessibility information. More space, lounge access, meals and refund flexibility are separate benefits; none should be assumed solely from the class name. If sitting together matters, check the actual reservation before paying.

Official sources: Eurostar

Food and drink

Look for the catering symbol and the offer for your train and class. A café, restaurant car, snack service and an included meal are not the same. Services can change with equipment or disruptions. Pack water and suitable food, especially for regional or late journeys, and check any operator restrictions.

Official sources: Eurostar

Luggage, bicycles and pets

European trains do not share one luggage allowance. TGV INOUI and Eurostar publish specific quantity or size rules, and low-cost products can charge for extra bags. Check the operator for your route, label bags, carry only what you can manage and keep aisles clear. Bicycles, pets and oversized items need a separate policy check.

Official sources: SNCF Connect · Eurostar

Wi-Fi and power

Wi-Fi, sockets and USB connections depend on the train and can fail. Download tickets and essential route details, carry a charged power bank and do not rely on streaming or a particular socket type. Check the facilities shown for the booked service rather than comparing unverified speed ratings.

Boarding and accessibility

Follow the arrival time and gate deadline on your ticket. Eurostar journeys to or from London involve pre-departure border and luggage checks; other Eurostar routes have different arrangements. Check travel documents with the relevant authorities. Arrange required assistance with the operator and verify the boarding point and station accessibility.

Official sources: Eurostar

Comfort and overnight travel

For a night train, compare a seat, couchette and sleeper, shared or private occupancy, bedding and any breakfast inclusion. Search the current Nightjet timetable rather than relying on old route names. For any long journey, keep valuables close, bring layers and water, and leave enough time for an onward connection.

Official sources: ÖBB Nightjet

Discount cards and families

Enter every traveller’s age and check the qualifying residence or card conditions. A youth, senior or family reduction is not universal. Calculate the card purchase cost against the saving on eligible fares, and check validity, automatic renewal and cancellation terms before buying a subscription.

Official sources: SNCF Connect · National Rail
