TGV Lyria: plan a France–Switzerland journey

Plan a France–Switzerland journey by comparing routes, travel classes, luggage rules and onward connections for your departure.

Choose the correct cross-border journey

TGV Lyria connects France and Switzerland, including journeys between Paris and Geneva, Lausanne, Basel or Zurich. Check the timetable for the exact date and stations. Published showcase journey times are not a substitute for the booking result, especially during works. In Paris, confirm the departure terminal rather than assuming all long-distance trains share one station.

Compare the actual class and conditions

Compare the actual classes, seat arrangements and included services displayed for your departure. Class labels and product benefits can evolve. Do not infer refund flexibility, a meal or lounge access merely from a first-class label. Check the fare’s exchange and refund conditions and keep the booking confirmation with its assigned place.

Luggage, meals and Swiss onward travel

Consult Lyria’s own luggage and bicycle instructions, not a generic French domestic allowance. Check current catering and any dietary needs before travel. For an onward Swiss journey, compare an integrated booking with separately purchased tickets and verify pass validity; a Swiss domestic travel product should not be assumed to cover the entire international train.

What to compare

Compare the same travel date, stations, passengers and accommodation. The cheapest result can become poor value once baggage, reservations, local transfers or restrictive changes are included. Save the complete offer and its conditions, not just the headline price.

Before you pay

Check the departure and arrival station names carefully. A city can have several stations, and a transfer across town needs more time than a change on adjacent platforms. There is no safe universal connection time: allow for luggage, children, access needs and the consequences of missing the next departure.

Connections and travel day

Keep your ticket, any separate reservation and required identity documents accessible offline. Recheck the operator’s travel information shortly before departure. If a disruption occurs, follow instructions for your actual booking and keep notifications and receipts; voluntary changes and disruption arrangements are different processes.

A practical final check

Before purchasing, write down the final cost, what is included, the change or cancellation deadline, and where help will come from. If an important condition is unclear, ask the seller before committing. A timetable entry is not a guarantee that a seat, berth or particular onboard facility is available.

Official sources

Independent planning guidance, not live availability or a fare quotation. Check the operator and your booking conditions for your travel date.
