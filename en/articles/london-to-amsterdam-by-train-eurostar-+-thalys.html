Practical route guide

London to Amsterdam by Eurostar: tickets, documents and station checks

Choose between a direct train and a connection via Brussels, then prepare for the correct London-route departure checks. Compare your actual travel dates, ticket conditions and pass reservations rather than relying on a headline fare or an old timetable.

1. Choose the stations and the complete itinerary

Search London St Pancras International to Amsterdam Centraal with your actual dates and every traveller’s age. Eurostar offers direct journeys and connections via Brussels-Midi/Zuid. Compare the whole itinerary: arrival time, number of changes, time at the interchange and onward local transport. Check the return separately; a direct outward option does not establish availability in the other direction.

This page preserves its historic URL containing “Thalys”. Thalys services now operate under the Eurostar brand, so the old name does not mean that you must buy an extra Thalys ticket. However, a continental Eurostar leg and a train to or from London have different boarding procedures and some different travel rules.

2. Complete passenger information and carry your documents

For journeys to or from the UK, Eurostar requires Advance Passenger Information (API) for everyone in the booking, including children. Complete it through the official Manage your booking service using details matching the travel document. A traveller’s ticket cannot be obtained until their information is complete; submitting API is not a substitute for carrying the actual document.

Check current passport, visa and entry-authorisation requirements for each traveller through the official travel-document guidance. National ID cards are accepted only in limited circumstances. Requirements depend on nationality, destination and individual status, so do not assume that a companion’s eligibility applies to you or that a rail ticket grants permission to enter a country.

3. Allow for London-route checks, in the right place

At London St Pancras International, use the recommended arrival time for your booking and allow for ticket, security and border checks before boarding. The train’s departure time is not the deadline for reaching the terminal. Download the current ticket close to travel and check service updates; do not apply the shorter arrival advice for Eurostar journeys wholly within continental Europe.

For a direct Amsterdam–London departure, follow the signs for the UK Terminal rather than waiting for an ordinary platform number. Use the arrival recommendation on your ticket. If you travel from Amsterdam to London with a Brussels change, follow the instructions for the continental first leg and the London departure process at Brussels. Check that the connection leaves time for those formalities, not just the walk between trains.

4. Prepare the right barcode at Amsterdam Centraal

On arrival by a direct train from London, scan the ticket barcode to leave Amsterdam Centraal. For a direct departure to London, Eurostar says to follow the UK Terminal route without scanning at the ordinary station ticket gates. For continental travel, including an itinerary to London via Brussels, use the ticket code at the station gates as instructed.

If your phone stores payment cards, save the train ticket in Apple Wallet or Google Wallet, or carry a printed ticket. Eurostar warns that a gate can otherwise read a stored bank card instead of an on-screen ticket image and charge it. Keep the booking reference and ticket accessible offline rather than relying on a working mobile connection at the gate.

5. Compare fare flexibility and luggage before paying

Compare the final total for the same party, itinerary and class. Read change deadlines, refund restrictions and the original seller’s servicing instructions. “No exchange fee” does not mean a later train costs the same: a higher new fare can still require payment of the difference. A promotional starting price is not availability for every departure.

Check the luggage allowance for every leg and class, especially when combining a London service with a continental train. You must be able to handle your bags safely. Verify bicycles, pets, oversized items and accessibility assistance before committing; a special-item service available on one Eurostar route is not automatically available throughout this journey. Keep doors and aisles clear when storing luggage.

6. Confirm pass-holder seats and a workable backup

Interrail lists a Global Pass as required for Eurostar trains to or from London, with a compulsory reservation in addition to the pass. Pass-holder places have limited availability. A train appearing in a timetable, or having normal tickets for sale, does not establish that a pass reservation is available. Check the specific train and permitted class before committing to the rest of a pass-based itinerary.

If pass-holder places are unavailable, compare another departure or an ordinary ticket rather than assuming the pass will let you board without a reservation. For a connection, check what your actual booking covers and what happens if the first train is delayed. Separate tickets may carry different protection from a through booking. Keep disruption messages and follow the operator’s instructions rather than treating voluntary-change rules as the answer to every disruption.
