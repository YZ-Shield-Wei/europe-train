Which train apps do you actually need in Europe?

Choose apps by the job they perform: buy and manage a ticket, follow the operating railway, or use a rail pass. One attractive journey planner does not replace all three.

Keep the seller and operator separate

The seller manages your order and its change or refund process. The operating railway provides journey-specific boarding and service information. If you buy from an agency, retain that agency's confirmation as well as the operator and train number. An agency app is not an official railway app.

Use railway tools for your route

DB Navigator offers DB journey information, ticket booking and reservation functions. For other routes, start from the official SNCF Connect, SBB or Trenitalia website to find the relevant app and supported services. Check that your exact ticket product and country are supported; a timetable result alone is not a purchasable fare.

Use Rail Planner correctly

Rail Planner is the companion for supported Interrail mobile passes. Follow the official activation and journey-adding instructions. Saving a journey does not reserve a seat, and a pass ticket does not replace a separate mandatory reservation. Keep reservation documents accessible alongside the pass.

Prepare for weak mobile reception

Open the required ticket before reaching the gate and follow the seller's instructions for offline access. Keep your phone charged and retain the order reference. A screenshot is not an accepted substitute for every product, especially where the app must display an active ticket. Check the pass's current internet requirements before departure.

Recheck on the day

Compare train number, departure station, platform and changes with the operator's current information and station displays. Cached timetables can be outdated. If two apps disagree, ask the carrier or station staff rather than assuming that the cheaper or earlier result is correct.

Install and share carefully

Use app links from the railway's own website or an official app store listing. Avoid sending booking barcodes or account passwords to strangers. For customer support, start from the seller's verified contact channel. This guide does not rank an app as universally cheapest or claim every railway can be booked in one place.
