Zurich to Milan by Train: Scenic Alpine Route Guide 2026

217km through the Alps - Gotthard Panorama Express, EC trains, and breathtaking scenery

Scenic Route 2026-07-25 8 min read

The train journey from Zurich to Milan is one of Europe's most spectacular rail routes. Covering 217 kilometers through the Swiss Alps and into northern Italy, this route offers two distinct experiences: the historic Gotthard Panorama Express with its winding mountain railways and panoramic windows, or the fast EuroCity (EC) trains through the world's longest railway tunnel.

Gotthard Panorama Express: The Scenic Choice

The Gotthard Panorama Express is a seasonal luxury train (May-October) that follows the historic Gotthard railway line with its famous loop tunnels and spiral bridges. Key highlights:

  • Panoramic windows from floor to ceiling for unobstructed Alpine views
  • Wassen Church loop - see the same church from three different heights
  • Gotthard Tunnel - 15km historic tunnel (or new 57km base tunnel on EC)
  • Lake Lugano approach - Mediterranean scenery before Italy

Booking tip: Reserve seats 2-3 months ahead in summer. The train runs daily from May to October, with reduced service in shoulder seasons.

EuroCity (EC): The Fast Option

The EC Zurich-Milan trains run every 2 hours throughout the day, offering:

  • Direct service with no changes required
  • Modern coaches with restaurant car
  • Power outlets and WiFi on most trains
  • First and second class options

The route passes through the Gotthard Base Tunnel (57.1 km), the world's longest railway tunnel, before emerging near Lugano and continuing to Milan via Como and Monza.

Station Guide

Zurich HB (Hauptbahnhof)

Switzerland's largest railway station with over 2,900 trains daily. The Zurich-Milan platforms are on the ground level. Allow 10 minutes to navigate from the main hall.

Milan Centrale

One of Europe's grandest stations, built in Art Deco style. The station connects to Milan Metro (M2 and M3 lines) and regional services throughout Lombardy. Walking distance to Duomo: 25 minutes or 3 metro stops.

Travel Tips

  • Best side for views: Sit on the right side (Zurich to Milan) for lake views
  • Border crossing: No passport checks within Schengen, but carry ID
  • Power plugs: Swiss (Type J) and Italian (Type L/L') - bring adapters
  • Food: Restaurant car available; pack snacks for budget travel
  • Luggage: Overhead racks and spaces between seats; no weight limit

Scenic Alternatives

If you have extra time, consider these detours:

  • Bernina Express - Add a day trip from Chur to Tirano (UNESCO World Heritage)
  • Glacier Express - Zermatt to St. Moritz, then connect to Milan
  • Lake Como stopover - Break journey at Como San Giovanni for lakeside scenery

FAQ

Do I need a reservation?

Reservations are compulsory on the Gotthard Panorama Express. For EC trains, recommended but not mandatory in Switzerland; seat reservations are advisable in summer.

Is the Swiss Travel Pass worth it?

If you're traveling extensively in Switzerland before/after Milan, the Swiss Travel Pass covers this route (seat reservation extra CHF 9-15). For a single trip, point-to-point tickets are cheaper.

Can I bring a bicycle?

Yes, but reservation required (CHF 10-20). Space is limited to 4-8 bikes per train. Book early in summer.

What's the best time to travel?

May-September for the Gotthard Panorama Express (winter service suspended). EC trains run year-round. Morning departures (8:00-10:00) offer the clearest mountain views.
