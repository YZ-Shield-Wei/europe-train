European train stations: a practical departure checklist

The city name is only the beginning. Confirm the station, boarding process and connection before travel so that a useful ticket becomes a manageable journey.

Find the exact station

Large cities have several stations with similar names. Compare the full station name and address in your booking with the operator's station guide. Check whether your next train leaves from the same station or requires a cross-city transfer; a city-wide search result does not remove that transfer.

Allow time for the actual boarding process

There is no single arrival-time rule for all European trains. Eurostar services to and from London involve pre-departure passport and baggage checks, unlike many other routes. Follow the arrival time on your current ticket and the operator's station instructions, rather than treating the gate-closing time as your arrival target.

Read the departure display

Match the train number and departure time, not only the final destination printed on the board. Platforms and train formations can change. Check coach and seat details when you have a reservation, and follow announcements or ask staff if the train divides or your coach is in a different part of the platform.

Make transfers realistic

Consider walking distance, stairs, lifts, luggage, children and any security or border checks. A short scheduled connection may not suit your group. Identify the protection of your through-ticket or separate tickets before booking, and leave more time when changing stations or catching the last train of the day.

Arrange assistance and facilities

Check accessibility and assistance booking requirements with the operator or station before travel. Do not assume every station has working lifts, lockers, a staffed ticket office or overnight access. The official station pages can help verify facilities, but opening hours and availability still need checking for your visit.

Keep the essentials ready

Have the valid ticket, required reservation, identity or discount documents and an accessible order reference. Complete any product-specific validation or activation before boarding. Keep luggage under your control and leave room to move safely. If something goes wrong, ask staff before boarding rather than relying on a universal onboard-ticket rule.

Official sources

Europe Train is an independent guide, not a railway operator. Rules can change; your ticket conditions and the operator’s current instructions take precedence.
