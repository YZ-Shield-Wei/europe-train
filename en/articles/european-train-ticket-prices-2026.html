How to Get the Cheapest Prices

1. Book Early (The #1 Rule)

Most European operators release tickets 3-4 months in advance. Early booking discounts:

  • SNCF (France): PREM'S fares up to 70% off, released 3 months ahead
  • Deutsche Bahn (Germany): Sparpreis from €19.90, released 6 months ahead
  • Trenitalia (Italy): Super Economy from €9.90, released 4 months ahead
  • Renfe (Spain): Promo fares from €15, released 4 months ahead
  • SBB (Switzerland): Supersaver up to 70% off, released 2 months ahead

3. Travel Off-Peak

Prices vary significantly by day and time:

  • Cheapest: Tuesday-Thursday, midday departures (10:00-14:00)
  • Moderate: Monday, Friday morning, Sunday evening
  • Most expensive: Friday evening, Sunday afternoon, holiday weekends

FAQ

Why are prices so different for the same route?

Prices depend on demand, booking time, and fare type. A Paris-London ticket can cost €49 (booked 3 months early) or €250 (booked same day).

Are budget fares refundable?

Generally no. PREM'S, Sparpreis, and Super Economy are non-refundable and non-changeable. Buy flexible fares if your plans might change.

Do children travel free?

Most operators allow children under 4 to travel free (no seat). Children 4-11 typically get 50% off. Family discounts vary by country.

Can I book tickets on the day of travel?

Yes, but you'll pay full fare. Budget fares sell out weeks in advance. For popular routes (Eurostar, TGV), book at least 2 weeks ahead.
