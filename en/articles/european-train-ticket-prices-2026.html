European train prices: build a realistic travel budget

There is no reliable fixed price table for every European route in 2026. Build a quote for your own dates and compare the complete trip, not an old promotional starting fare.

Sources checked: 30 October 2026

Define the same journey

Use the same date, stations, class, passenger ages and discount eligibility in every comparison. Record the train number and whether the itinerary is direct or changes trains. A cheaper journey may arrive at a different station, take longer or offer less flexibility.

Compare the full payable amount

Separate the travel fare from seat reservations, sleeping accommodation, local transfers, bicycle places and seller fees. Check the checkout total and currency before paying. An optional service should not become part of your budget without understanding what it provides.

Treat low starting fares as availability-dependent

A promotion is not a promise for every departure or passenger. Search alternative times if your itinerary allows it, but do not assume that buying on a particular weekday guarantees a discount. When sales have not opened, a missing price is not proof that the route is unavailable.

Compare a pass on equal terms

Add the pass price, mandatory reservations and supplements for the actual trains you want. Then compare with individual tickets for the same journey. Check travel-day allowance and geographic coverage. A pass can offer flexibility, but it is not automatically cheaper or a guarantee of space on a reserved train.

Budget for your group's needs

Families, groups, bicycles and overnight accommodation have product-specific rules. Enter the actual travellers instead of multiplying one adult promotional fare. If you may change your plans, compare the price of flexibility with the cancellation conditions and potential replacement-ticket cost.

Keep a dated quote

Save the time of the search, seller, fare conditions and final amount. Compare currency conversion and payment fees where relevant, without assuming one seller always has the best exchange rate. Recheck before payment; this page provides a method, not live prices or a guaranteed saving.
