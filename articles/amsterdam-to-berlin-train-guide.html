Amsterdam to Berlin by Train: Complete Guide 2026

Published: July 11, 2026 | Route Distance: 575 km

The Amsterdam to Berlin train route connects two of Europe's most vibrant capitals through scenic Dutch and German countryside. With ICE International and InterCity services running daily, this journey offers a comfortable alternative to flying.

Route Overview

The direct train from Amsterdam Central to Berlin Hauptbahnhof covers approximately 575 kilometers. ICE International trains complete the journey in about 6 hours and 20 minutes, while InterCity connections may take up to 7.5 hours with one transfer, typically at Hannover or Osnabruck.

Train Services

ICE International (Direct)

The flagship service operated jointly by NS (Dutch Railways) and DB (German Railways). These modern high-speed trains run 6 times daily, offering:

  • Journey time: 6h 20m
  • 1st and 2nd class seating
  • Free WiFi throughout
  • Power outlets at every seat
  • On-board restaurant car
  • Quiet zones available

InterCity with Transfer

Budget-conscious travelers can take an InterCity train with one connection, usually in Osnabruck or Hannover. Total journey time ranges from 7 to 7.5 hours.

Station Information

Amsterdam Central (Amsterdam Centraal)

  • Located in the city center, walking distance to main attractions
  • Connected to metro, tram, and bus networks
  • Facilities: luggage lockers, restaurants, shops
  • Arrive 15-20 minutes before departure for international trains

Berlin Hauptbahnhof

  • Europe's largest crossing station, opened in 2006
  • Direct connections to U-Bahn (metro), S-Bahn, buses, and trams
  • Facilities: extensive shopping, dining, luggage services
  • Located near government district and Tiergarten

Travel Tips

  • Border crossing: No passport checks within Schengen, but carry ID
  • Seat reservation: Recommended but not mandatory; costs €4.50 extra
  • Best scenery: Sit on the right side (Amsterdam to Berlin) for Dutch windmills and German countryside
  • Power outlets: Available at all seats on ICE International
  • Food: Restaurant car available; or bring your own food and drinks

Alternative Routes

If direct trains are sold out or expensive, consider:

  • Amsterdam -> Dusseldorf -> Berlin (slightly longer but more frequent)
  • Overnight option: Amsterdam -> Hannover (hotel) -> Berlin next morning

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a seat reservation?

Seat reservations are optional on this route but recommended, especially during peak travel times (Fridays, Sundays, holidays). Reservation costs €4.50 in 2nd class.

Can I use a Eurail Pass?

Yes, Eurail Global Pass and Select Pass (including Germany and Netherlands) cover this route. However, seat reservations are mandatory on ICE International and cost approximately €5.90.

Is there WiFi on the train?

Yes, ICE International trains offer free WiFi throughout the journey. InterCity trains also have WiFi, though quality may vary.

How early should I book?

For the cheapest Sparpreis fares, book 2-3 months in advance. Tickets are available up to 180 days before departure.
