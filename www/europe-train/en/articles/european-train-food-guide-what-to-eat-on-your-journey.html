European Train Food Guide: What to Eat on Your Journey

One of the joys of European train travel is experiencing the diverse culinary traditions of each country—right from your seat. From French bistro cars to German currywurst at stations, this guide covers everything you need to know about eating well on European trains.

Onboard Dining by Country

🇫🇷 France (SNCF)

  • TGV InOui: Café-bar serving sandwiches, salads, and pastries
  • TGV Lyria: Premium dining with hot meals and wine selection
  • Must-try: Croissant and café au lait for breakfast
  • Tip: Bring a baguette and cheese from local shops for authentic experience

🇩🇪 Germany (DB)

  • ICE: BordRestaurant with full-service dining
  • Menu: Schnitzel, sausages, pretzels, and German beers
  • Must-try: Currywurst with fries at major stations
  • Tip: First class includes complimentary snacks and drinks

🇮🇹 Italy (Trenitalia)

  • Frecciarossa: Executive class includes gourmet meals
  • Bar Car: Panini, espresso, and Italian wines
  • Must-try: Prosciutto and mozzarella panini
  • Tip: Bring your own wine—it's allowed and common!

🇨🇭 Switzerland (SBB)

  • Dining Car: High-quality Swiss cuisine with regional specialties
  • Must-try: Rösti (Swiss potato dish) and chocolate
  • Tip: Glacier Express and Bernina Express include meal service in the fare

🇪🇸 Spain (Renfe)

  • AVE: Cafeteria car with Spanish tapas and wines
  • Preferente Class: Complimentary meal and drinks
  • Must-try: Jamón ibérico and manchego cheese

🇬🇧 UK (National Rail)

  • LNER: At-seat catering with British classics
  • GWR: Pullman dining on selected routes
  • Must-try: Afternoon tea service on some trains
  • Tip: Bring your own food—it's a British tradition!

Bring Your Own (BYO) Guide

Bringing your own food is not only allowed on most European trains—it's often encouraged. Here's how to do it right:

Best Foods to Pack

  • Sandwiches: Easy to eat, minimal mess
  • Fresh Fruit: Apples, grapes, berries (avoid messy fruits)
  • Cheese and Crackers: A European classic, perfect with wine
  • Nuts and Dried Fruit: Healthy, non-perishable snacks
  • Chocolate: Swiss or Belgian for a sweet treat
  • Water Bottle: Refillable bottles are eco-friendly; most stations have fountains

Foods to Avoid

  • ❌ Strong-smelling foods (kimchi, durian, etc.)
  • ❌ Messy foods (soups, saucy pasta)
  • ❌ Noisy packaging (crinkly bags)
  • ❌ Anything requiring heating (unless dining car available)

Station Food Highlights

Some train stations are destinations in themselves for food lovers:

  • Paris Gare du Nord: Grab a croissant from a nearby boulangerie
  • Munich Hauptbahnhof: World-famous Christmas market food in season
  • Roma Termini: Pizza al taglio (by the slice) stands
  • Zurich HB: Swiss chocolate shops and gourmet food hall
  • London King's Cross: The famous Platform 9¾ shop and nearby cafés

Dietary Considerations

  • Vegetarian/Vegan: Most operators now offer plant-based options; BYO is safest bet
  • Gluten-Free: Limited options onboard; pack your own bread and snacks
  • Halal/Kosher: Very limited; bring your own meals for long journeys
  • Allergies: Always inform staff; ingredient lists may not be available

Budget Tips

  • Buy food at supermarkets before boarding (Aldi, Lidl, Carrefour)
  • Avoid onboard dining cars for budget travel (markups are 30-50%)
  • Bring a reusable water bottle and fill at stations
  • Pack a small cooler bag for perishables on long journeys
  • Share a bottle of wine with travel companions (allowed and common!)

Special Dining Experiences

  • Glacier Express (Switzerland): Gourmet meal served at your seat while crossing the Alps
  • Orient Express (Various): Luxury dining experience (€200+ per person)
  • Bernina Express (Switzerland-Italy): Regional specialties with panoramic views
  • Night Trains: Breakfast included with sleeper cabins

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I bring alcohol on trains?

Yes, on most European trains. Beer and wine are commonly consumed. Spirits may be restricted on some operators. Drink responsibly!

Is there a microwave I can use?

Generally no. Bring cold foods or purchase hot meals from the dining car.

Can I order food delivery to the station?

Yes! Many stations have Deliveroo, Uber Eats, or local equivalents. Order during your journey and pick up at the station.

What's the best train for food lovers?

The Glacier Express in Switzerland offers the finest onboard dining, while Italian trains have the best casual food culture.

Do I need to tip onboard staff?

Tipping is not expected but appreciated. Round up to the nearest euro or leave 5-10% for exceptional service.
