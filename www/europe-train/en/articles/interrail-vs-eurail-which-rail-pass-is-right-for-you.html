Interrail vs Eurail: Which Rail Pass is Right for You?

Planning a European rail adventure? Choosing between Interrail and Eurail can be confusing. This guide breaks down the key differences, eligibility rules, pricing, and helps you pick the perfect pass for your journey.

What Are Interrail and Eurail Passes?

Both Interrail and Eurail are flexible rail passes that allow unlimited travel across Europe's extensive train network. They cover 33 countries and most major train operators, including high-speed services like TGV, ICE, and Frecciarossa.

When to Choose Interrail

  • You live in Europe (EU, UK, Iceland, Norway, Switzerland, Turkey)
  • You want flexibility to hop on and off trains
  • You're planning multiple trips within Europe
  • You value spontaneous travel over fixed itineraries

When to Choose Eurail

  • You're visiting Europe from outside the continent
  • You want a comprehensive multi-country experience
  • You're planning an extended European tour (2+ weeks)
  • You want to avoid individual ticket purchases

What's Included

  • ✅ Unlimited travel on participating railways
  • ✅ Free travel on some scenic routes (Glacier Express, Bernina Express)
  • ✅ Discounts on ferries (Greece-Italy, Sweden-Poland)
  • ✅ Free or discounted city transport in some cities
  • ✅ Discounts on selected hotels and attractions

What's Not Included

  • ❌ Seat reservations (€10-€50 per journey for high-speed/night trains)
  • ❌ Some premium trains (Eurostar, Thalys/欧洲之星红线 may require supplements)
  • ❌ Private railways not part of the network

Booking Tips

1. Reserve Early for High-Speed Trains

TGV, ICE, and Frecciarossa require reservations. Book these as soon as you know your travel dates, especially during summer.

2. Download the Rail Planner App

Both Interrail and Eurail offer free apps to check timetables, plan routes, and book reservations on the go.

3. Consider Your Travel Style

If you prefer staying 3-4 days in each city, a Flexi pass is more economical. If you want maximum flexibility, go Continuous.

4. Check for Promotions

Both passes often have seasonal discounts (up to 20% off). Black Friday and early spring are good times to buy.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use Eurail in my home country?

No, Eurail is designed for international travel. Your home country is excluded from coverage.

Do children need a pass?

Children under 4 travel free (no pass needed). Ages 4-11 get a free pass when traveling with an adult (max 2 children per adult).

Can I upgrade to first class?

Yes, first-class passes are available at a premium. They offer more spacious seating, quieter cars, and sometimes complimentary meals.

What if I miss my reserved train?

Reservations are typically for specific trains. If you miss it, you'll need to make a new reservation (additional cost may apply).

Is the pass worth it?

Calculate your planned routes individually. If the total exceeds the pass price, it's worth it. For 3+ countries or 10+ travel days, passes usually save money.
