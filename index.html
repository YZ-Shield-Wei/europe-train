TICKETS & SEATS 01

Do I need a reservation?

A rail pass is not a seat reservation. Some trains require a separate booking, often at extra cost.

Check before booking +
  • Check the reservation requirement for every train, not just the route.
  • For a pass journey, confirm reservation availability before committing to the trip.
  • Keep your ticket or pass and any required reservation ready for inspection.
Interrail reservation guidance →
TICKETS & SEATS 02

Can I change my plans?

“Train ticket” is not one set of rules. Change and refund conditions depend on the fare you buy.

Read the fare conditions +
  • Check the change deadline, any fee and whether you must pay a fare difference.
  • Check if a refund is allowed and how it is paid.
  • Keep the booking confirmation and use the seller's instructions to make changes.
DB saver-fare cancellation rules →Eurostar refund guidance →
PLANNING 03

Will my connections work?

Check the exact stations and transfer details. A city name alone is not enough to plan a connection.

Build a realistic itinerary +
  • Check whether you change platforms, stations or transport modes.
  • Allow for luggage, unfamiliar stations and your own mobility needs.
  • Before buying separate tickets, ask the seller what happens if a delay causes a missed connection.
Look up your journey with DB →
PLANNING 04

Thinking of a night train?

Choose the accommodation, not just the departure. A seat, couchette and sleeping cabin are different products.

Choose your overnight journey +
  • Check which accommodation is offered on your chosen service and date.
  • Confirm whether the compartment is shared or private, and what is included.
  • If travelling with a pass, check the additional booking required for your accommodation.
Explore official Nightjet options →
PLANNING 05

Need travel assistance?

Check the whole journey: boarding, station access and transfers, as well as the train itself.

Arrange support in advance +
  • Contact the operator's assistance team before booking if you need help choosing a suitable service.
  • Check assistance booking deadlines for every country on your journey.
  • Confirm wheelchair spaces, boarding arrangements and transfer times for your needs.
DB accessible travel & assistance →
TRAVEL DAY 06

Is my train running?

Use your operator's current service information. This guide does not display a live departure feed.

Check your actual service +
  • Look up your travel date, train number and departure station.
  • Check service notices before leaving and recheck station displays when you arrive.
  • If a service changes, follow the operator's instructions before buying a replacement ticket.
